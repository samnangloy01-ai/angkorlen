## Notes

### 1. Prasat Chrung Southeast (ប្រាសាទជ្រុង-អាគ្នេយ៍)
- Location: owner's Google Maps pin 13.4279237, 103.8732482 (13°25'40.5"N 103°52'23.7"E). SE corner of the Angkor Thom wall, Sangkat Nokor Thum.
- Records (helloangkor HA12857): Khmer name on helloangkor បា្រសាទជុ្រង(ទិសអាគ្នេយ៍); MH 364 (1926), IK 472.01, CISARK 1718. Remains: outer enclosure, entry gopura, stele shelter, sanctuary. 20-min walk on the wall from the South Gate; sunrise spot.
- Stele K.597: Sanskrit, all 4 faces (A–D), Saka 12th c., poor condition (Coedès, IC IV p. 231).
- APSARA: all 4 Prasat Chrung built end of Jayavarman VII's reign, Bayon style, cruciform sanctuary + square stone pavilion; carvings of small celestial beings; pediments to Lokesvara and Buddhas; SE one best preserved; 1,500 m walk from the gate.
- Not on page: the conversion to Hindu worship under Jayavarman VIII (it is on the NE page from earlier sources; APSARA's page does not mention it — not repeated here). Content of K.597 not summarised (no source read).

### 2. Gate of the Dead (ខ្លោងទ្វារខ្មោច)
- Location: owner's Google Maps pin 13.4414661, 103.8732166 (13°26'29.3"N 103°52'23.6"E), added 24 Nov 2026. Cover changed to sf-gatedead-3b (gate behind leaves) at owner's request.
- helloangkor HA12151: Thvear Khnoch / Khmoch; IK 473.02, CISARK 1009, MH 363; asuras/devas causeway remains (some likely in moat silt); two laterite chambers either side (purpose unknown); medallion band; original sandstone paving with wheel ruts; not on modern road network; statues found nearby by Henri Marchal 1925 (Hevajra, Prajnaparamita head, Buddha head, two Avalokitesvara); wall walk 7 min to Victory Gate, 18 min to SE Prasat Chrung. Refs: BEFEO Chronique 25 & 33; Rapport de la Conservation d'Angkor, May 1925.
- Wikipedia (Angkor Thom): east gate = Gate of the Dead; Victory Gate 500 m north; 23 m face towers; Bayon style, Jayavarman VII.
- Name origin: sources disagree (funeral processions / defeated armies / simply the east entrance) — page says it is uncertain.
- Lonely Planet: Tomb Raider filming location.
- Steel cables around the face tower: from the owner's photos only (no source found on who/when) — page only says they hold the blocks.

### 3. Prasat Daun Chan (ប្រាសាទដូនចាន់)
- Location: owner's Google Maps pin 13.4740231, 104.2219017 (13°28'26.5"N 104°13'18.8"E). Boeng Mealea village/commune, Svay Leu district.
- helloangkor HA11595: IK 215, CISARK 308, MH 302 (1926), JB 258; laterite enclosure, east cruciform gopura, sandstone shrine (decayed, never finished), library SE, basin NE (perhaps S); hospital chapel attributed to Jayavarman VII (no inscription cited); Lajonquière's lintels not seen now. Aymonier 1900 p.458; Lajonquière 1902 p.285.
- 102 hospitals: Tourism Cambodia (Chapel of the Hospital page).
- NOT on page: helloangkor says "around 400 m SW of the western entrance of Beng Mealea", but the owner's pin is ~1.3 km west of Beng Mealea's west side — conflict, so the page only says "west of Beng Mealea".
- Wooden door frames: from owner's photos only.

### 4. Spean Thma (ស្ពានថ្ម)
- Location: owner's Google Maps pin 13.446191, 103.8795661 (13°26'46.3"N 103°52'46.4"E). ~200 m east of Thommanon (Lonely Planet, Tourism Cambodia, Angkor Guide).
- Wikipedia (Rooney 2005 p.313): former course of Siem Reap River between Angkor Thom and East Baray; probably rebuilt after the Khmer period (~15th c.), reused sandstone blocks; 14 arches 1.10 m wide.
- Angkor Guide (Glaize): piers 1.60 m; cleared by H. Marchal 1920; bridge about twice as wide as the river.
- helloangkor HA11676: IK 491, MH 381 (1926 "ruined bridge"), CISARK 392; blocks with Bayon-era carving; helloangkor gives "17th century".
- Penang travel tips: "16th century". Phnom Penh Post: "originally constructed in Angkorian times". → Date conflict; page says sources disagree.
- National Geographic (Dan Penny): eastern end destroyed; floods; river went around the bridge.
- Ta Keo nursery waterwheel nearby (Phnom Penh Post, helloangkor).
- Lonely Planet says "an arch and several piers remain" vs. 14 arches elsewhere — page says "the remains show 14 arches" (Glaize/Wikipedia).
