AngkorLens (www.angkorlens.com) is a personal photography and heritage website about Cambodia's Khmer temples. This page explains, in plain words, what information the site uses and the rules for using its content.

Cookies and browser storage

ឃុកឃី និងការរក្សាទុកក្នុងកម្មវិធីរុករក

AngkorLens does not use advertising cookies. Google Analytics is used only if you accept it (see below). To make the site work, it saves a few small settings in your own browser ("local storage"). They stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • whether you closed or accepted the cookie notice;
  • whether you signed up for the newsletter, so the sign-up box does not keep appearing;
  • which temple maps you have opened (for the free map limit);
  • small display choices, such as hiding a notice you have closed.

You can remove these at any time by clearing your browser's site data for angkorlens.com.

Google Analytics

If you click Accept on the cookie notice, AngkorLens uses Google Analytics (Google tag GTM-KRG6W6QM) to count visits and see which pages are popular, which countries visitors come from and how they find the site. Google may set cookies for this. If you click Close or do not accept, Google Analytics is not loaded. To change your choice, clear your browser's site data for angkorlens.com and choose again. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Some content is loaded from other services, which may receive basic technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser contacts them: photos are delivered through the image service wsrv.nl, and links open Google Maps, WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, Facebook or Instagram. Those services follow their own privacy policies.

Newsletter sign-up

ការចុះឈ្មោះទទួលព័ត៌មាន

If you enter your email address in the sign-up box, it is sent to the owner of AngkorLens through the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), only so that we can send you AngkorLens updates. We do not sell or share your email address. To stop receiving updates or to ask us to delete your address, contact us using the details below.

Tour bookings

When you book a tour, your request is opened in WhatsApp or Telegram and sent by you. We use the details you give (name, date, number of guests, pickup place) only to arrange your tour.
