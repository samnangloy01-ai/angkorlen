The view from Coit Tower

ទិដ្ឋភាពពីលើប៉ម Coit Tower

Telegraph Hill · 1933

Photographs by Samnang Loy · AngkorLens

📍 The view from Coit Tower, San Francisco, CaliforniaOpen in Google Maps · បើកផែនទី
Where: Top of Telegraph Hill, San FranciscoCompleted: 1933

Coit Tower is a tall, round white tower on top of Telegraph Hill. From the windows at the very top you can see the whole city and the bay all around you.

To the west, the Golden Gate Bridge stretches across to the green Marin hills, with Russian Hill and the Fisherman’s Wharf waterfront below. To the north, Alcatraz Island sits in the middle of the bay, with Angel Island behind it.

To the south, the towers of the Financial District stand close together: the pointed Transamerica Pyramid, the rounded Salesforce Tower, and the Bay Bridge reaching across the water past the Ferry Building clock tower.

Look closely: The Transamerica Pyramid · the Golden Gate Bridge far away with fog on the hills · Alcatraz Island · the Ferris wheel at Fisherman’s Wharf · the round parking circle at the foot of the tower, far below.
